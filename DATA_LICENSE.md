# Data licences and attribution

CartCast YYC does not redistribute raw data. `cartcast fetch` downloads it from the
publishers each time, and the built dashboard shows derived figures with attribution.

| Data | Publisher | Licence | Attribution shown in the dashboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly average retail prices for selected products, Table 18-10-0245-01 | Statistics Canada | [Statistics Canada Open Licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/reference/licence) | Yes (footer) |
| Consumer Price Index, monthly, not seasonally adjusted, Table 18-10-0004-01 | Statistics Canada | [Statistics Canada Open Licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/reference/licence) | Yes (footer) |
| Calgary Business Licences (`vdjc-pybd`) | The City of Calgary | [Open Government Licence – City of Calgary](https://data.calgary.ca/d/Open-Data-Terms/u45n-7awa) | Yes (footer) |
| Canada's Food Price Report 2026 (outlook range only) | Dalhousie University and partner universities | Cited, not redistributed | Yes (footer) |

Required wording for StatCan-derived products: *"Adapted from Statistics Canada,
[table title and number]. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics
Canada of this product."*

## Sample data

`data/sample/` is **synthetic** and is generated by `scripts/make_sample_data.py`.
Each price series is anchored on the real Alberta average price for June 2024 and
then simulated. Store names are fictional (`SAMPLE …`). The dashboard shows a
"Sample data" banner whenever it is built with `--sample`. Never present sample
figures as real.
