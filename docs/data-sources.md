# Data sources

## 1. Monthly average retail prices for selected products: StatCan Table 18-10-0245-01

- **Page:** <https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810024501>
- **Download used:** `https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/18100245-eng.zip`, which contains `18100245.csv` and `18100245_MetaData.csv`
- **Geography:** Canada, provinces, Whitehorse and Yellowknife. CartCast uses `GEO = "Alberta"`. There is no Calgary-level price.
- **Frequency:** monthly, published about 5–6 weeks after the reference month.
- **Method:** built from retailers' scanner (point-of-sale) data. Products are a specific size (e.g. `Eggs, 1 dozen`) or standardised to one (e.g. `Block cheese, 500 grams`), and fresh produce and meat are per kilogram.
- **Columns used:** `REF_DATE` → `month`, `GEO`, `Products` → `product`, `VALUE` → `price`
- **Gotchas:**
  - Some cells are suppressed (blank `VALUE`). The loader drops them and the dashboard carries the last price forward.
  - Product names occasionally change when StatCan updates the basket. `cartcast validate` catches this and suggests the closest names.
  - StatCan cautions against using average prices to compute change over time (see methodology).

## 2. Consumer Price Index, monthly, not seasonally adjusted: StatCan Table 18-10-0004-01

- **Page:** <https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810000401>
- **Download used:** `https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/tbl/csv/18100004-eng.zip`. It's a large file, so it is streamed in chunks.
- **Filters:** `GEO ∈ {"Alberta", "Calgary, Alberta"}`, products `All-items` and `Food purchased from stores`, `UOM = 2002=100`
- **Derived:** 12-month % change (`yoy`) = index / index 12 months earlier − 1
- **Use:** the "grocery inflation" KPI. This is the correct measure of food inflation.

## 3. Calgary Business Licences: City of Calgary Open Data (`vdjc-pybd`)

- **Page:** <https://data.calgary.ca/d/vdjc-pybd> · **API:** `https://data.calgary.ca/resource/vdjc-pybd.json` (Socrata SoQL)
- **Fields used:** `tradename`, `address`, `comdistnm` (community), `licencetypes`, `jobstatusdesc`, `point` (GeoJSON lon/lat)
- **Query:** licence type contains `RETAIL DEALER - PREMISES`, status `Licensed` or `Pending Renewal`, and the trade name matches a grocery keyword (see `config/settings.yaml → stores`).
- **Classification:** `supermarket` if the name matches a chain/supermarket keyword, otherwise `specialty`. Names matching exclusion keywords (e.g. "flea market") are dropped.
- **Limitations:** licences have no "grocery" category, so keyword matching will miss some stores and catch a few non-grocers. Tune the keyword lists, and mention this in the presentation as a data-quality trade-off.

## 4. Canada's Food Price Report 2026

- **Page:** <https://www.dal.ca/sites/agri-food/research/canada-s-food-price-report-2026.html>
- **Used for:** the outlook band only. Overall food prices are forecast to rise 4% to 6% in 2026 (`dashboard.outlook_annual_low/high`).
- **Context figures for the pitch:** the average family of four is expected to spend $17,571.79 on food in 2026, up to $994.63 more than 2025. Food prices are 27% higher than five years ago, and Alberta is among the provinces forecast above the national average.

## Sample dataset (`data/sample/`)

Synthetic, generated by `scripts/make_sample_data.py` (seeded, reproducible). Files
match the real layouts exactly. Price series are anchored on the real Alberta June
2024 average prices and then simulated. Store names are `SAMPLE …`. The dashboard
shows a banner whenever sample data is used.
