## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-09
### Added
- Data pipeline: StatCan 18-10-0245-01 (prices) and 18-10-0004-01 (CPI), and City of Calgary business licences (`vdjc-pybd`).
- `cartcast` CLI with `fetch`, `validate`, `build` and `serve` commands.
- Interactive static dashboard with editable basket, household sizes, smart swaps, price movers, aisle shares, store map, outlook band and shareable basket links.
- Offline sample dataset and generator.
- Test suite, ruff linting, CI, and a monthly refresh workflow that deploys to GitHub Pages.
- Design mockup, documentation and presentation plan.
